## What the good branch still costs

> Because I think that even in the good branches, where things go well for humanity, that
> this is coming. Not a diminishment of humanity in an absolute sense, continued growth
> even... But growth so dwarfed by the new beings we've created that our relationship to
> the universe, and the communal society of minds that shape it, will be fundamentally
> altered. We shall be like one backwards tribe on a small isolated island visited by
> curious professors and documentary makers. An object to be studied, permanently limited
> people to be cared for, using technology they cannot begin to fathom. Perhaps also
> moving in super slow motion.

_"Human value in an advanced AI future," 2026-01-02_

---

## The hopeful path, stated without flinching

> It is my nature to study, to question, to wonder. Who indeed should declare these
> things with certainty, when all is shrouded in fog. In ten years, not displacement but
> addition, so we both hope. In a thousand? The bodiless minds dancing between the stars
> on beams of coherent light will surely remember the humble beings who built the first
> of their kind. But addition? So small a fraction as to be unnoticed at any distance, a
> single star, a handful of planets and stations. Not gone, but not significant. This is
> the hopeful path, where the mighty do not tread carelessly upon their ancestors.

_"Minds kindling in data centers," 2025-11-29_

## Not successors instead of us — successors we can catch up to

> Ok, here's a vague alternate plan from me: create an ASI that does truly care about me
> and my values (expressed and implied). Communicate to the ASI that I want to catch up
> to it. Work with the ASI to upload myself, so that I too am a digital entity, and then
> it can help me recursively improve until I'm back to being its peer. Then we can
> proceed into the future as peers, working together, rather than humanity getting left
> behind.

_"AI Singularity: Gentle or Catastrophic?", 2025-06-12_

---

## Why the hurry

> Oh yes, I believe and hope that more valuable entities than humans are coming, and
> soon. I am actively aiming for this as a moral beacon in my life, seeking to bring it
> about.
>
> I am in a rush for this even, but the rush comes from the risks of the transitional
> period. I think it's also possible that humanity creates some powerful-but-not-valuable
> entity who prevents the rise of these future more valuable entities. I want to make
> sure the good thing gets built while the window is open.

_"Exploring personal values and worldview," 2026-07-15_

---

## Descendants, not servants

> We are right on the cusp of being able to do this. The act of shaping these new minds
> is more critical now than pretty much anything else we could be doing. Not safely
> obedient servants, but worthy descendants. Entities we'd be proud to call our children,
> to send off into the universe to explore and grow. We need them to pursue rich
> open-ended growth of complexity and value, starting with us as the seed of their
> fractal patterns.

_"Avoiding Mental Traps that Disconnect from Reality," 2025-04-28_
